1. dg-it-point-launch.mp4 — 9:16 portrait, H.264/AAC, web-optimized 720x1280.
2. dg-it-point-community-thank-you.mp4 — 9:16 portrait, H.264/AAC, web-optimized 1080x1920.

Playback behavior:
- Videos autoplay muted, loop, and play inline only while their cards are visible.
- Each card has accessible custom Pause/Play and Sound on/off buttons.
- Turning sound on for one video automatically mutes the other video.
- Videos are not loaded until playback is requested/visibility triggers play, and poster images are shown before video frames are ready.
- Browser, battery-saving, data-saving, and operating-system policies can still restrict autoplay or audio.

SEO note:
- The videos are embedded in the homepage and the page provides descriptive titles and context.
- VideoObject schema or a video sitemap has not been fabricated because verified publication/upload dates and standalone watch-page information have not been supplied. Add those only if accurate data and a suitable video-indexing setup are available.

If replacing either MP4 later, keep the correct filename, portrait 9:16 aspect ratio, compatible H.264/AAC encoding, and update the poster to a representative frame.
